This is a placeholder essay. It exists so the article surface has something real to
render — and so the reading experience being built here has a witness. Everything
described below is engineered already or specified in the plan; it arrives after the
hub ships.

## The page you are reading, eventually

Articles here will be set the way this one is — Spectral on lit paper, a measure that
respects your eyes, night mode that inverts the material rather than repainting it.
Then the parts that earn the visit:

**Diagrams that look like LaTeX and behave like instruments.** Figures drawn in the
site's hairline ink, cross-wired to the prose: highlight a term and the figure lights
its counterpart; touch a curve and the paragraph that explains it answers.

**Footnotes that come to you.** References surface beside the text on highlight —
no jumping, no losing your place.

**Marginalia.** Short asides living in the margin where they belong.

**A table of contents that knows where you are** and travels with the page's one
continuous line.

**Code you can edit and run.** C and C++ compiled in your browser by a real clang,
in a worker, warm compiles around two hundred milliseconds. Editable with vim
bindings, rendered like part of the page rather than an IDE that crashed into it.

**Mathematics** set beautifully, sharing the same highlight wiring as the diagrams —
because equations and figures are the same argument in two scripts.

## Why a placeholder says this much

Because the bar for this site is that nothing on it is filler. Even the stand-in
essay states its intent, is marked as a sample, and will be replaced by writing that
does what this page only promises.
